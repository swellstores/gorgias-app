Answer "where's my order?" without leaving the ticket. This app connects Gorgias to your Swell store, so tickets from your customers open with their Swell profile and recent orders beside them, and every customer in Swell shows whether they're waiting on support.

When a ticket comes in, Gorgias checks with your store. The app matches the customer to their Swell account and sends back who they are and what they've bought, which Gorgias shows in the ticket sidebar. The ticket is recorded on that customer in Swell at the same time, so the rest of your team can see who has an open conversation without opening Gorgias.

- **Know who you're talking to.** A Swell customer card in the Gorgias sidebar shows the customer's name, order count, total spent and how long they've been buying from you. Agents can tell a first-time buyer from your best customer before they reply.
- **Recent orders, one click from the details.** The customer's five latest orders are listed with number, status, date and total. Each one links straight to the order in your Swell dashboard, and the customer card links to their account.
- **Support history in Swell.** A Support tab on every customer shows their open ticket count, their latest ticket and a link to it in Gorgias. An Open tickets column on the customer list shows who's waiting on an answer before you contact them.
- **Customers Gorgias already knows.** Your customers exist in Gorgias before they write in, with their name, email and phone number, linked to their Swell account. They're kept up to date as accounts change and orders are placed, paid, canceled, refunded, shipped and delivered. When you connect, existing customers who have ordered come over too, newest buyers first, up to 100, 500 or 1,000.
- **Nothing to build in Gorgias.** Connecting creates the integration and sidebar widget in your Gorgias account for you. Run it again after changing a setting and it updates them in place instead of adding duplicates.

**Ticket status, if you want it.** By default the app hears about new tickets. Turn on ticket updates and it also hears when a ticket is closed or reopened, so open-ticket counts in Swell stay accurate. It's off by default because it means more traffic between the two systems.

**Secured with a secret you choose.** Calls between Gorgias and your store must carry a shared secret you set, and signed requests from Gorgias are verified before the app reads or writes anything.

Setup takes a few minutes. Enter your Gorgias subdomain, a REST API key and the email of the agent it belongs to, choose a shared secret and turn on sync. Then run the one-time connect step, and the next ticket from a Swell customer opens with their orders in the sidebar.
