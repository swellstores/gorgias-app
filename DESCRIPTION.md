Answer "where's my order?" without leaving the ticket. This app connects Gorgias to your Swell store, so tickets from your customers open with their Swell profile and recent orders beside them, and every customer in Swell shows how many open tickets they have.

When a ticket comes in or changes, Gorgias checks with your store. The app matches the customer to their Swell account and sends back who they are and what they've ordered, which Gorgias shows in the ticket sidebar. The ticket is recorded on that customer in Swell at the same time, so the rest of your team can see open conversations without opening Gorgias.

- **Know who you're talking to.** A Swell card in the Gorgias sidebar shows the customer's name, email, order count, total spent and how long they've been buying from you, and links to their account in your Swell dashboard. Agents can tell a first-time buyer from your best customer before they reply.
- **Orders with their tracking.** The customer's five latest orders are listed with number, status, date and total, the items in each, and tracking numbers once they've shipped. Each order links straight to it in your Swell dashboard.
- **Open tickets in Swell.** A Support tab on every customer shows their open ticket count and their latest ticket, with a link to it in Gorgias. An Open tickets column on the customer list shows who's waiting on an answer before you contact them.
- **Customers Gorgias already knows.** Turn on customer sync and your customers are created in Gorgias with their name, email and phone number, linked to their Swell account, and updated when they change their details or place an order. Existing customers who have ordered come over too, newest buyers first, up to 100, 500 or 1,000.
- **Nothing to build in Gorgias.** The app creates the integration and sidebar widget in your Gorgias account for you, and updates them in place when you change a setting.

**Current by default.** The app hears about new tickets and about changes to existing ones, so open-ticket counts and the sidebar stay current. You can limit it to new tickets to cut traffic, but then closed tickets keep counting as open.

**Nothing to copy.** You approve access in Gorgias itself, so there are no API keys to find or paste, and you can disconnect from either side at any time.

Setup takes a minute. Open Gorgias in your Swell dashboard's menu, enter your Gorgias subdomain, click Connect Gorgias and approve access. You're brought back to Swell, and the next ticket from a Swell customer opens with their orders in the sidebar.
